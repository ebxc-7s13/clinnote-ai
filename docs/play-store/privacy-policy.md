ClinNote ("the app") is a clinical documentation and evidence-review assistant for healthcare professionals. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

## 1. Information stored on your device

Patient records you create (optional name, date of birth or age, sex, patient reference), consultation transcripts, clinical facts, notes, reports and cached reference information are stored **only on your device**, encrypted with a key held in Android secure storage. We do not operate a server that stores this information, and Android cloud backup of the app is disabled.

## 2. Microphone and speech recognition

The app uses the microphone only while you are recording a consultation, after you start it. Speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service on your device. Where the device supports on-device recognition, the app requests it. Otherwise, your device's speech service (on most devices provided by Google) may process the audio under that provider's terms. Temporary audio, if kept for transcription, stays in the app's private cache and is deleted after the transcript is created, and in any case within 24 hours. The app creates no voiceprints.

## 3. Public reference sources

When you view evidence or medication information, the app sends **clinical terms only** (for example a medication or condition name) over an encrypted connection to public sources: U.S. National Library of Medicine services (RxNorm, DailyMed, PubMed, MedlinePlus, ClinicalTrials.gov, PubChem, Clinical Tables), openFDA and Europe PMC. Names, dates of birth, patient references and transcript sentences are never sent.

## 4. What we do not do

No account is required. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software. We do not sell data or use it for advertising. We do not receive your patient records.

## 5. Sharing and exports

Information leaves the app only when you export or share it (PDF, text or JSON). Exported copies are outside the app's protection and are your responsibility.

## 6. Deletion

You can delete a visit, a patient, or all app data in Settings. Deleting all data also destroys the encryption key. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the device.

## 7. Security

Data at rest is encrypted; network connections use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 8. Intended users and children

The app is intended for healthcare professionals and is not directed to children.

## 9. Medical disclaimer

ClinNote is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. For medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## 10. Changes

We will update this policy when the app's data handling changes and change the effective date above.
