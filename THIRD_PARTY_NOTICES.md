## 6. Public data sources

ClinNote displays records fetched at run time from public services; it does not bundle their content. Each record links to its source, and the content remains subject to the publisher's terms:

- U.S. National Library of Medicine — RxNorm/RxNav, DailyMed, PubMed/NCBI E-utilities, MedlinePlus, PubChem, Clinical Tables, ClinicalTrials.gov (https://www.nlm.nih.gov/web_policies.html).
- U.S. Food and Drug Administration — openFDA, Drugs@FDA (https://open.fda.gov/terms/). openFDA states: "Do not rely on openFDA to make decisions regarding medical care."
- Europe PMC / EMBL-EBI (https://europepmc.org/Copyright).

Use of these services does not imply endorsement of ClinNote by NLM, NIH, FDA, EMBL-EBI or any other organization.
